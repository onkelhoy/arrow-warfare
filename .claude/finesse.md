# Finesse

Small touches for late, after the game works. Not planned yet, just kept so they don't get lost.

## Touch controls
- **The joystick stays as it is**: it appears under the thumb wherever you press on the left. The owner likes it a lot.
- **Option to pin the joystick** to a fixed spot (for touch only devices), in case a player gets confused by a stick that moves around.
- **Or a faint hint instead**: tiny hands shown faintly on screen, showing where to put your thumbs (move on the left, bow on the right).

## World
- **Gnomes**: an arrow that flies out of bounds gets thrown back in by a gnome a few seconds later, landing near the edge and pointing in. Just a fun idea, not a planned feature. Until then the forest wall catches the arrows.
